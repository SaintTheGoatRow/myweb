 Project Overview
This repository contains an enhanced, responsive, and interactive personal website developed for ICT251 Activity 3. It includes semantic HTML5 elements, modular external CSS, fluid media components, and client-side JavaScript features.

---

  Interactive JavaScript Features

  1. Contact Form Validation & Local Preview (Compulsory)
*  Description:** Performs client-side validation on the contact form using `preventDefault()`. Rejects blank, whitespace-only entries, or incorrectly formatted email addresses.
* How to Test:** 
   1. Scroll down to the **Contact Me** section.
   2. Try submitting with blank fields or only spaces to observe field error messages.
   3. Enter valid details and click **Send Message** to display a local preview of the input without refreshing the page.

  2. Theme Switcher (Light / Dark Mode)
*  Description:** Toggles between a light background and a high-contrast dark background with white text across all cards and section containers.
* How to Test:** Click the **🌙** button located in the top navigation header.


  3. Weekly Study Hours Calculator
   Description:** Calculates weekly study time based on user-entered hours per day and active days per week, rejecting zero or invalid ranges (outside 1–7 days).
  How to Test:** Scroll to **My Learning Plan**, enter valid numbers into the inputs, and click **Calculate Hours**.

  4. Expandable Content (Hobbies Detail Toggle)
   Description:** Dynamically attaches toggle buttons to hobby cards, allowing visitors to show or hide additional workflow details with clear open (`▲ Hide Details`) and closed (`▼ Show More Details`) states.
  How to Test:** 
   1. Scroll to the **My Hobbies** section.
   2. Click **▼ Show More Details** on any hobby card to expand extra content.
   3. Click **▲ Hide Details** to collapse the text back.
